## Original LinkedIn Post

Most banks are asking, “What AI feature should we launch next?”

The better question is: “Can AI agents safely use our bank?”

There’s a meaningful difference between an AI-enabled bank and an AI-enabling bank.

AI-enabled banks add copilots, smarter onboarding, better fraud models, and cleaner customer experiences. Necessary work, but increasingly easy to copy.

AI-enabling banks become trusted infrastructure for agents acting on behalf of customers and businesses. That means secure permissions, verifiable identity, clear liability, real-time controls, auditable transactions, and APIs built for machine-to-machine use.

The next banking advantage will not come from the best chatbot.

It will come from becoming a dependable part of the AI operating stack.

For fintech executives, this changes the product roadmap. For bank leaders, it changes the investment case. The question is no longer just how AI improves your front end. It is whether your institution can be trusted when an agent needs to move money, validate a counterparty, or execute within a customer’s defined limits.

The institutions that solve that trust layer will own a much more valuable position than a feature.

Is your bank building AI features, or becoming part of the AI operating stack?
